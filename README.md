# nanjing-medical-guide
CareCompanionNJ - Hospital companion service for university students in Nanjing

CareCompanionNJ is a student-led initiative connecting international university students in Nanjing (with specialized support for Indonesian students) with empathetic medical student companions from Nanjing Medical University (NJMU) for hospital visits.

🌟 How the Platform Works

Advance Booking (3-7 Days): Students submit requests 3–7 days in advance with their symptoms and campus location in English or Indonesian.

Platform Pre-Payment: Requests are validated once payment is made directly to the manager/platform.

Curated Escort Job Board: Hand-picked, trusted NJMU medical students review available requests and choose shifts that fit their schedule and campus location. (Patient booking fees remain completely confidential to the manager).

Smart Hospital Recommendation: NJMU companions assist patients in navigating the Chinese hospital system and advise on the best hospital (e.g., Jiangsu Province Hospital, Sir Run Run Shaw Hospital, NJMU Eye Hospital).

Hospital Escort: Companions assist with physical navigation, triage procedures, registration, and basic doctor-patient communication support (excluding clinical procedures, prescription management, or insurance paperwork).

Verified Payout: Escorts submit completion proof and user confirmation to release their stipend.

✨ Key Features

Bilingual EN / ID Interface: Full support for both English and Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia) student audiences.

Founder & Student Trust Profile: Personal credibility section highlighting NJMU student leadership.

Interactive Booking & Fee Calculator: Calculates fees based on half-day or full-day sessions and campus pickup options.

Escort Request Board Preview & Application: Private board simulation with completely hidden patient pricing, plus an application flow for prospective vetted escorts.

Curated Hospital Guide: Tailored directory of top student-visited hospitals in Nanjing.

WeChat Direct Booking & QR Code Modal: Easy connection with the platform manager.
