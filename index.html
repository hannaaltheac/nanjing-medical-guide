Why I Started ConnectCareNJ

A Trusted Peer Support Network Built by a Medical Student

As a medical student in Nanjing, I’ve seen firsthand how overwhelming it can be for international students—especially our Indonesian student community in Nanjing—when they get sick. Even for students who speak decent everyday Chinese, navigating hospital triage, registration counters, and complex multi-floor diagnostics is stressful when you are unwell.

I founded ConnectCareNJ to offer supportive peer accompaniment. All our escorts are hand-picked fellow medical students who possess student empathy, basic medical understanding, and familiarity with hospital routines. We don't claim to be professional medical interpreters, but we are knowledgeable peers who stand by your side so you never have to go through a hospital visit alone.

All our companions are active medical students. Advance booking allows them to review incoming requests, coordinate shift availability around their lectures or clinical rotations, and avoid schedule conflicts.
Included: Physical hospital accompaniment, line/counter navigation, helping you communicate symptoms to doctors with medical understanding, and recommending suitable hospitals.
Excluded: Escorts are peer helpers (not certified professional medical translators). They do not issue official medical advice, perform clinical procedures, manage WeChat registration accounts, or handle insurance paperwork.
You pay the booking fee to the founder to confirm your request. The amount you pay is never shown to companions. After the visit is completed, the companion receives their pre-arranged stipend.
Companions are not random public applicants. They are hand-picked, empathetic, kindhearted medical students selected directly by the founder. Every application is reviewed before shift board access is granted.
Cancel more than 48 hours before your visit: full refund.
Cancel 24–48 hours before: 50% refund.
Cancel less than 24 hours before: no refund.

Companion cancels or does not show up: full refund or a free reschedule.
Not included: hospital registration fees, tests, medication, and your own transport. You pay these directly at the hospital. The campus pick-up fee is non-refundable once your companion has set off.
