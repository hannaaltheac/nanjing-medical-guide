Why I Started CareCompanionNJ

A Trusted Peer Support Network Built by an NJMU Student

As a medical student at Nanjing Medical University (NJMU), I’ve seen firsthand how overwhelming it can be for international students—especially our Indonesian student community in Nanjing—when they get sick. Even for students who speak decent everyday Chinese, navigating hospital triage, registration counters, and complex multi-floor diagnostics is stressful when you are unwell.

I founded CareCompanionNJ to offer supportive peer accompaniment. All our escorts are hand-picked fellow medical students from NJMU who possess student empathy, basic medical understanding, and familiarity with hospital routines. We don't claim to be professional medical interpreters, but we are knowledgeable peers who stand by your side so you never have to go through a hospital visit alone.
